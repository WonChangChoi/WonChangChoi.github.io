01 / About

I turn interfacial spin interactions into controllable functionality.

My research centers on the electrical control of artificial spin configurations through spin-orbit torque and exchange bias. By combining thin-film growth, nanofabrication, magnetotransport, and magneto-optical imaging, I connect nanoscale spin alignment with measurable magnetic and transport properties.

I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Physics and Chemistry at DGIST, where I conducted my doctoral research in the Spin Nanotechnology Laboratory under the supervision of Prof. Jung-Il Hong.

02 / Selected Research

Selected work

From creating helical spin textures to translating them into nonvolatile electrical responses.

01

Published · APL Materials 2023

90° helical spin alignment

Created artificial helical spin structures in AFM/FM/AFM trilayers through spin-orbit-torque control of exchange bias, then electrically selected chirality and tracked its effect on domain-wall dynamics.

Helical spinsExchange biasKerr microscopy
02

In press · Advanced Functional Materials 2026

Ferrimagnetic spin engineering

Tuned the compensation temperature of Pt/IrMn₃/CoGd multilayers by up to ~110 K through electrical control of the ferrimagnetic spin configuration. Selective exchange coupling between the IrMn₃ layer and the Co sublattice of CoGd enables interfacial spin control to modify the ferrimagnetic spin alignment and thereby tune the compensation temperature.

FerrimagnetismSpin-orbit torqueCompensation temperature
03

Manuscript in preparation

Uniform ↔ 180° helical switching

Realized reversible electrical switching between uniform and 180° helical spin states in Pt/NiO/Ni/NiO multilayers, producing nonvolatile resistance states and distinct spin-wave dynamics.
